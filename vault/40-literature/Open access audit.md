# Open access audit of `paper/refs.bib`

Checked 2026-09-23. Sources: Crossref licence metadata, OpenAlex location records, DataCite (arXiv DOIs), and the
publisher, repository or author page itself (each free copy was downloaded or its landing page opened and its title
checked). "Open" = the venue is open access or its proceedings are free (NeurIPS, ICML/PMLR, AAAI, IJCAI, JAIR, JMLR,
TMLR/ICLR on OpenReview, ACL Anthology), or the entry is an arXiv preprint or a software/web resource. "Closed" = the venue
is subscription-based (a hybrid journal counts as closed even when the individual article is OA; that case is recorded as
a free version on the publisher site).

free-version field** (4 arXiv `eprint`s, 14 `note = {Open version: \url{...}}`), and **3 have no verified free version**
(`omahony2008cphydra`, `kadioglu2010isac`, `lodi2013variability`).

## Citation policy (Berk, 2026-09-23)
Cite only what a reader can legally read in full: open venue, or a verified preprint / author copy pointed to in the
reference. A reference with neither is dropped unless it is crucial — then it is discussed with Berk first.
Status: all 35 cited references comply; 15 arXiv preprint links are printed in the bibliography, the rest are open venues
or carry an "Open version" link. Works with no free version (omahony2008cphydra, kadioglu2010isac, lodi2013variability and the prior-art finds) live in
[[Paywalled research]] and are cited only in the paper's appendix subsection `app:paywalled`. Open question for Berk: Rice 1976 is available free only as the 1975 Purdue technical report.
