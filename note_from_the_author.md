# Note from the Author

`This is the only part that is not touched by AI`

We are now past the verge of a revolution and perhaps we shall merge the terms and call this period "revolutions per second", borrowing the term from the rotation industry.

There were always standards in research. Some of which are good documentation, reproducibility, a hypothesis, research steps, experimentation, explicit checks, declaration of contributions and failures. 

To be honest, most research papers fail on more than one, sometimes all the criteria (yet still get published). Most include other skills such as "cherry picking" if not honest failings.

This work is prepared as an after thought. I had the compute, I had the AI subscriptions (Claude, ChatGPT and some local LLMs) and I had the idea (for some time). So, I said why not?

I love research, I have cooler feelings about academia in general. I also don't like research behind 50-100 USD paywalls per paper (this is another discussion). All the latest developments are towards the betterment of research but to the detriment of academia as in its current shape.

By all means, this is at least a "passable" research as a grad work and perhaps even publishing worthy in one of the SCI. What makes this a great work is the algorithmic speed and capability. In 2026, agentic AI has leapt forward in an unprecedented way promoting from the "copilot" to the "pilot".

The main experiment in this study is whether a "not so frontier research" can be conducted by agents. In the current state, I think it is a great way of publishing in SCI journals if your buddies are on the editorial board. Or you can rubber stamp grad students in the current era.

My opinion, after this small experiment is that academic research shall be conducted on a whole different level. All other tiers are just human education. It also means infrastructure and hardware (or subscriptions) are now must items to run the AI agents on experiments.

I roughly guess that performing the experiments and preparing this resarch would be weeks if not months of grad work, just two years ago. It took me only 2 days alongside my day job and other extra work, net attention is probably less than 3 hours.

As far as I am concerned, my curiosity is initially satisfied. This is true academic work with a few extras. Fully reproducible, ready for other agents to pick up from where it started or use in their research as input (no more parsing pdf files) code is clean and ready. It has tutorials for humans, too. Hallucination risk is low because it is solver work and outputs are measurable. Fully auditable of course.

I believe that this is the future of research and AI agents are in the center. The future is exciting..
